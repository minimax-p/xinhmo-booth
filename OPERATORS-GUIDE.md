# Staff guide

Everything you need to run the booth at an event. Print it and keep it at the
booth.

## Before guests arrive

1. Plug in the **camera** with its **power adapter**, not just the battery.
   Switch it on and take the lens cap off. The lens switch stays on **AF**.
2. Plug in the **printer**, switch it on, and check it has paper and ink.
3. On the Mac, double-click **START-BOOTH**.
4. It checks the camera and the printer first. If something is wrong it says
   exactly what to do: follow the steps, press **Return**, and it checks again.
5. When both say **OK**, the booth opens on its menu: *Tap a layout to begin*.

Leave the small black Terminal window open behind the booth. It is what keeps
the booth running.

> **The staff phone.** Join the phone to the same Wi-Fi as the Mac, then open
> the address the booth shows (for example `http://10.11.20.247:8080`) and
> enter the staff code. Bookmark it. Everything below is done from the phone.

## How a session goes

| Guest does | Booth shows |
| --- | --- |
| Taps a layout: Grand, Trio or Quad | A short get-ready screen |
| Poses | 6 photos, a 7-second countdown each, *Hold still* as the camera fires |
| Taps their favourite photos | Anything they skip is filled in for them |
| Picks a frame design and a look | Keychains, charms and extra copies can be added here |
| Taps **Done** | A **pickup code**, for example `B6W` |

After each group the booth **locks itself**, so the next group cannot walk into
someone else's session.

## Your job: the phone

### When a group finishes

1. They tell you their **pickup code**.
2. Take payment. It is Zelle or cash only, and extra copies are $3 each.
3. On the phone's **Orders** tab, tap their code, then the blue **Print**
   button (it says how many photos, and the amount to be paid). A print
   takes about a minute.
4. Hand over the print.
5. Tap **Start next session** at the top of the Orders tab to let the next
   group in.

### Keychains and charms

Each keychain prints **two identical strips**, one for each side, and **one
sheet holds two keychains**. Cut along the thin lines; the pickup code is
printed under each strip, so a pile of them is easy to sort.

In the order, tap **Print keychains** (it says how many). Someone who comes back later for more:
open their order, change the number with **+**, and print again. The price
updates by itself.

> **Grand has no keychains or charms.** A Grand print is one big sheet, so there
> is no strip to cut them from. The booth's menu says so, and the phone greys
> them out on Grand orders.

### Paper and ink

The printer's paper tray holds **18 sheets**, and an ink cassette prints **36**.
The booth counts both. The **Booth** tab shows how many are left, and a banner
appears when they run low.

After you reload, **tap Paper or Ink** on the phone, so the count starts again.

## The Booth tab

| Control | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| **Restart camera** | The camera shows as off or stops answering |
| **Test print** | You want to check the printer: it reprints the last sheet |
| **Calibration sheet** | Print edges look cut off; the person who set up the booth uses this |
| **End current session** | A group walked off halfway through |
| **Shut down booth** | The event is over |

The top of the tab says which camera driver is running. It should say
**Canon SDK**.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Phone shows **Camera off** | Is the camera on? Is the power adapter plugged in, or the battery charged? Turn it **off, wait 5 seconds, on**. Then tap **Restart camera**. |
| Booth says *No camera found* | Same as above. |
| Photos are blurry | People moved, or stood too close to the camera. Ask them to stand on the floor mark. More light helps. |
| A print does not come out | Check the paper tray and the ink. Turn the printer **off for 5 seconds, then on**. |
| Phone banner: **prints are waiting** | The printer has stopped. Same fix as above. |
| Phone says **Offline** | The phone lost the Wi-Fi. Reconnect it to the same network as the Mac. |
| Red banner: **Test mode** | The booth is not really printing. Call whoever set the booth up. |
| Booth screen is frozen or black | Wait 30 seconds; it restarts itself. If not, close the black window and double-click **START-BOOTH**. |
| Booth says it is out of storage | The Mac's disk is nearly full. Call whoever set the booth up. |

> **No phone?** Hold the **top-left corner** of the booth screen for **2
> seconds** and enter the staff code. The same controls are there.

If you cannot fix something, take a photo of the screen and note the time. The
booth keeps a log that can be read afterwards.

## Turning it off

1. On the phone's **Booth** tab, tap **Shut down booth**, then confirm.
2. Switch off the printer and the camera.
3. Shut down the Mac.

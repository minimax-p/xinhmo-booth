# Photobooth: staff guide

Print this page and keep it at the booth.

## Turning it on

1. Plug in power, the camera cable, and the printer cable.
2. Turn on the **camera** (check the lens cap is off).
3. Turn on the **printer**.
4. Turn on the **computer**.
5. Double-click **START-BOOTH**.
6. Wait about 20 seconds. When you see **Tap to start**, it is ready.

Do not close the small black text window that opens. Leave it behind the booth
screen. It is what keeps the booth running.

## Turning it off

1. Press and hold the **top-left corner** of the screen for 3 seconds.
2. Type the staff code.
3. Tap **Shut down booth**, then confirm.
4. Turn off the printer, the camera, and the computer.

## During the event

Customers do everything themselves. Your job is to keep an eye on three things:

- **Paper and ink.** One pack prints 108 photos. Both run out at the same time,
  so when the paper is gone, change the ink cassette too.
- **Prints piling up.** Hand them over so the tray does not overflow.
- **The camera staying awake.** If the camera screen goes dark and photos stop
  working, switch the camera off and on again.

## If something goes wrong

Press and hold the top-left corner for 3 seconds and enter the staff code. The
staff screen shows whether the camera and printer are working, and has buttons
to check again, restart the camera, and send a test print.

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Screen is frozen or black | Wait 30 seconds; it restarts itself. If not, close the black text window and double-click START-BOOTH again. |
| "No camera found" | Check the camera is on and the cable is pushed in at both ends. Then tap **Restart camera** on the staff screen. |
| "Another program is using the camera" | Tap **Restart camera**. If it keeps happening, restart the computer. |
| "The camera could not focus" | Check the switch on the lens is set to **MF**, not AF. |
| Photos taken but nothing prints | Check the printer is on and has paper. Tap **Test print** on the staff screen. |
| "The printer is out of paper or ink" | Load a new paper and ink pack. |
| Printer light is flashing | Open the printer, check for a jammed sheet, close it, and try a test print. |
| Everything looks fine but nothing works | Turn the computer off and on, then double-click START-BOOTH. |

If you cannot fix it, take a photo of the screen and send it along with the day
and time. The booth keeps its own log that can be read afterwards.

## Practising without using up paper

Double-click **TEST-MODE** instead of START-BOOTH. The booth then works normally
but uses pretend photos and does not print anything. Good for training. Close the
window when you are done and start it normally for real customers.

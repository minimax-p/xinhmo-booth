XINHMO BOOTH -- START HERE
==========================

You need: this Mac, the Canon camera, the SELPHY printer, and the Mac's
login password. The very first time, also an internet connection.


EVERY EVENT
-----------

1. Plug in the camera and the printer. Switch both ON.
   Use the camera's power adapter, not just its battery.

2. Double-click  START-BOOTH.command

   It checks the camera and the printer first. If something is wrong it
   says exactly what to do -- follow the steps, press Return, and it
   checks again. When both say OK, the booth opens by itself.

   Leave the black Terminal window open while the booth runs.

3. On the staff phone, open the address the booth shows and enter the
   staff code. Tap the Booth tab to see the camera, printer, paper and ink.


JUST WANT TO CHECK THE CAMERA AND PRINTER?
------------------------------------------

   Double-click  CHECK-BOOTH.command

   Same check, without opening the booth. It can print a test page too.


THE FIRST TIME ON A NEW MAC
---------------------------

   a. Moved this folder over by zip, AirDrop or USB stick? macOS may
      refuse to open it. Open Terminal, type this, then a space, drag
      this folder onto the Terminal window, and press Return:

        xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine

   b. The EDSDK folder must be inside this folder. It is Canon's camera
      software: licensed, never on GitHub, so it is copied across by hand.

   c. Double-click START-BOOTH.command. The first time, macOS may say it
      "cannot be opened": right-click it > Open > Open. Only once.

      Setting up takes 5-15 minutes, by itself:
      - It asks for this Mac's password. Nothing shows while you type.
        Press Return.
      - If a window asks to install Apple's "command line developer
        tools", click Install, then double-click START-BOOTH again.


IF SOMETHING IS OFF
-------------------

   Printer does nothing:
     System Settings > Printers & Scanners > add the printer.
     Its name must match "printerName" in settings.json.

   Staff code: "staffPin" in settings.json (it is 1234 -- change it).

   Everyday running: OPERATORS-GUIDE.md

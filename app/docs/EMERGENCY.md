# Emergency

For when something goes wrong and Minh is not there. Most problems are fixed
in a minute with the first section. The rest is for when Minh sends a fix from
afar.

## First, the quick fixes

1. **Look it up in the staff guide.** The table *If something goes wrong* in
   **OPERATORS-GUIDE** covers the camera, the printer, the phone and a frozen
   screen.

2. **Restart the booth.** On the staff phone's **Booth** tab, tap **Shut down
   booth**. Then double-click **START-BOOTH**. If the screen is frozen, close
   the black Terminal window instead, then double-click START-BOOTH.

3. **Check the camera and printer.** With the booth shut down, double-click
   **CHECK-BOOTH** and follow its steps.

## Telling Minh

Text Minh:

- a photo of the booth screen, and of the black Terminal window
- what happened just before, and what time
- whether a group was in the middle of a session

Never delete, move or rename anything in the **app** folder. The photos and
orders are in there.

## When Minh sends a fix

Minh can send a new version of the booth from anywhere. Putting it in takes a
minute and keeps the staff code, the settings, the photos and the orders.

1. **Shut down the booth.** On the phone's **Booth** tab, tap **Shut down
   booth**. No phone? Hold the **top-left corner** of the booth screen for 2
   seconds, then tap **Shut down booth**.

2. **Make sure this Mac has the internet.** If the venue's Wi-Fi has none,
   turn on a phone's **Personal Hotspot** and join the Mac to it.

3. **Open the EMERGENCY folder and double-click `UPDATE`.** If macOS says it
   *cannot be opened*, right-click it, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.

4. **Read what it says** and do what the table says:

   | It says | What to do |
   | --- | --- |
   | **UPDATED** | The fix is in. Go to step 5. |
   | **NO NEW UPDATE** | Minh's fix has not arrived yet. Tell Minh, wait a minute, and double-click UPDATE again. |
   | **COULD NOT REACH THE INTERNET** | Go back to step 2, then double-click UPDATE again. |
   | **THE BOOTH IS STILL OPEN** | Go back to step 1. |
   | **THE UPDATE DID NOT WORK** | Send Minh a photo of the window. Nothing was changed, so the booth still works as before. |

5. **Start the booth.** If you joined a hotspot just for the update, put the
   Mac back on the Wi-Fi the staff phone uses. Then double-click
   **START-BOOTH** and try one session yourself.

## If the update made things worse

Open the EMERGENCY folder and double-click **`UNDO-UPDATE`**. It puts the booth
back exactly as it was before the update, and needs no internet. Then
double-click **START-BOOTH**, and tell Minh.

> **Only update when Minh asks you to.** Never in the middle of a session:
> shut the booth down first, and the update tells you if you forgot.
